# Holo Play, the look

Holo Play looks like Prism Foundry: warm near-black, quiet type, one accent.

- Theme: the `hologram` preset (`app/design/theme.json`), unchanged. Archivo for display, Geist for text.
- One accent: the brand orange marks the play button and nothing else.
- Words: a section has a title and no description. A control that an icon explains has no label; its name is in
  its tooltip and for screen readers.
- Artwork leads. Tiles have no borders; the picture is the tile. A missing picture is the icon of what it stands for.
- The player says one true thing about quality, in one quiet line: the format playing and how it is routed.
- Modes: light, dark and immersive come from the preset. The page has no colour of its own.
