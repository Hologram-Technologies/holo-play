# The Prism design system

Generated by prism-code from its catalogue (`prism.design/1`); `prism-code design --json` prints the same as data, with your app's theme.

A page is plain HTML in `app/view/`. Its look is said only in the catalogue's words: components on ordinary elements with `data-slot`, and utilities with Tailwind's names bound to the brand's tokens. The build compiles `prism.css` from what the page uses, and `prism-code check` refuses anything outside it, naming what to use instead. The page is authored, not proved: the model decides, the page shows.

## The head of every page

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="prism.css">
<script src="prism-ui.js"></script>
<script type="module" src="prism.js"></script>
<script type="module" src="app.js"></script>
```

The build writes `prism.js`, `prism.css`, `prism-ui.js`; a page may not hold its own. Scripts never `import` each other: a holospace inlines every file. Ask the model with `const { invoke } = globalThis.prism; const answer = await invoke(text);`. The model decides every answer; the page shows it and never holds a rule (R1).

## Components

### Layout

- **app** on `<div>` or `<body>`.The whole-screen shell of an app: a column that fills the window, holding a toolbar and the app below it. Put one <main> inside it.
  `<div data-slot="app"><header data-slot="toolbar">…</header><main class="flex-1">…</main></div>`
- **page** on `<div>` or `<main>`.A centred column of content with the side margins of the brand, for pages that read top to bottom. Hold one <main>, or be it.
  `<main data-slot="page" class="grid gap-6 py-8">…</main>`
- **toolbar** on `<header>` or `<div>` or `<nav>`.The bar across the top of an app or a pane: title on the left, actions on the right. Stays at the top as the page scrolls. As <header>, it is the banner; give it the app's <h1>.
  `<header data-slot="toolbar"><h1 class="text-base font-semibold">Budget</h1><button data-slot="button" data-size="sm">New</button></header>`
- **split** on `<div>`.Two panes side by side, a sidebar and the main pane; on a phone the sidebar sits above. Fills the height it is given. Variants: data-variant: sidebar, even. The main pane is the <main>; the sidebar is an <aside> or <nav> with a label.
  `<div data-slot="split"><aside data-slot="sidebar" aria-label="Conversations">…</aside><main>…</main></div>`
- **sidebar** on `<aside>` or `<nav>` or `<div>`.The narrow pane of a split: a list to choose from, navigation or filters. Label it (aria-label) when there is more than one navigation region.
  `<aside data-slot="sidebar" aria-label="Chats"><ul data-slot="list">…</ul></aside>`
- **scroll-area** on `<div>` or `<section>` or `<ol>` or `<ul>`.A region that scrolls on its own, with a quiet scrollbar. Give it a height through its layout (flex-1 in a column). Variants: data-stick: top, bottom. Focusable (tabindex="0") when it holds no control, so keyboard users can scroll it; give it a label.
  `<div data-slot="scroll-area" class="flex-1" tabindex="0" aria-label="Messages">…</div>`
- **brand** on `<a>` or `<div>` or `<span>`.The app's wordmark: a small mark and its name, top left of the toolbar or sidebar. Every app has a name; give it one. The mark is aria-hidden; the name is text.
  `<a data-slot="brand" href="#"><span data-slot="brand-mark" aria-hidden="true"><span data-slot="icon" data-icon="scissors"></span></span><span data-slot="brand-name">Atelier Rose</span></a>`
- **brand-mark** on `<span>` or `<div>`.The wordmark's mark: an icon or a letter on the primary colour.
  `<span data-slot="brand-mark" aria-hidden="true">A</span>`
- **brand-name** on `<span>` or `<strong>` or `<h1>`.The app's name in the display face.
  `<span data-slot="brand-name">Ledger</span>`
- **page-header** on `<header>` or `<div>` or `<section>`.The top of a page: a small eyebrow line, the title in the display face, one line under it, and the page's actions to the right. The title is the page's h1 (or h2 under an app-level h1).
  `<header data-slot="page-header"><p data-slot="eyebrow">November 2026</p><h1>Sales overview</h1><p>Everything the team closed this month.</p></header>`
- **eyebrow** on `<p>` or `<span>` or `<div>`.A small uppercase line above a title: a date, a section, a step.
  `<p data-slot="eyebrow">Step 2 of 3</p>`
### Content

- **icon** on `<span>` or `<i>`.One of the catalogue's icons (lucide, ISC), named by data-icon, drawn in the current text colour at the current text size. prism-code design --json lists them. Variants: data-size: md, sm, lg, xl. Beside a word, aria-hidden="true". Alone in a button, the button gets the aria-label.
  `<span data-slot="icon" data-icon="trash-2" aria-hidden="true"></span>`
### Layout

- **hero** on `<section>` or `<header>` or `<div>`.The opening of a landing or home screen: an eyebrow chip, a large display headline, one paragraph, and the call to action with a quiet second link. Put a card beside it with grid utilities. The headline is the page's h1.
  `<section data-slot="hero"><span data-slot="badge" data-variant="chip"><span data-slot="icon" data-icon="sparkles" aria-hidden="true"></span>Proved by Lean</span><h1>Budget, settled.</h1><p>Every total is checked by the model before you see it.</p><div class="flex items-center gap-6"><a data-slot="button" data-size="xl" href="#add">Add an expense <span data-slot="icon" data-icon="arrow-right" aria-hidden="true"></span></a><a data-slot="button" data-variant="link" href="#how">How it works</a></div></section>`
### States

- **tip** on `<aside>` or `<div>`.A one-line hint across the foot of a page: an icon, a short title, one sentence and a link at the end.
  `<aside data-slot="tip"><span data-slot="icon" data-icon="sun" aria-hidden="true"></span><strong>Tip</strong><p>Drag a card onto a pile, or press Enter twice.</p></aside>`
### Layout

- **separator** (shadcn/ui) on `<hr>` or `<div>`.A hairline between groups. Variants: aria-orientation: horizontal, vertical. <hr> is a separator already; a <div> needs role="separator".
  `<hr data-slot="separator">`
### Content

- **card** (shadcn/ui) on `<section>` or `<div>` or `<article>` or `<form>`.A surface that groups one thing: a form, a chart, a list. Holds card-header, card-content and card-footer. Variants: data-variant: default, muted. A <section> with aria-labelledby naming its card-title is a region a screen reader can jump to.
  `<section data-slot="card" aria-labelledby="t"><div data-slot="card-header"><h2 data-slot="card-title" id="t">Spending</h2><p data-slot="card-description">This month</p></div><div data-slot="card-content">…</div></section>`
- **card-header** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<header>`.The top of a card: title, description and an optional action to the right.
  `<div data-slot="card-header">…</div>`
- **card-title** (shadcn/ui) on `<h2>` or `<h3>` or `<h4>` or `<div>`.A card's title. A heading at the page's next level. Keep headings in order.
  `<h2 data-slot="card-title">Totals</h2>`
- **card-description** (shadcn/ui) on `<p>` or `<div>`.One quiet line under a card title.
  `<p data-slot="card-description">This month</p>`
- **card-action** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>`.An action at the top right of a card's header.
  `<div data-slot="card-action"><button data-slot="button" data-variant="ghost" data-size="sm">Edit</button></div>`
- **card-content** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<form>` or `<ul>` or `<ol>`.The body of a card.
  `<div data-slot="card-content">…</div>`
- **card-footer** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<footer>`.Actions or totals at the foot of a card.
  `<div data-slot="card-footer"><button data-slot="button">Save</button></div>`
- **stat** on `<div>` or `<section>`.One figure with its label, and an optional change under it. For dashboards and totals. Variants: data-tone: default, success, destructive, brand.
  `<div data-slot="stat"><p data-slot="stat-label">Left this month</p><p data-slot="stat-value">€1,885</p></div>`
- **stat-icon** on `<span>` or `<div>`.A stat's icon, in a bordered tile at its top right.
  `<span data-slot="stat-icon" aria-hidden="true"><span data-slot="icon" data-icon="dollar-sign"></span></span>`
- **stat-delta** on `<p>` or `<span>`.How the figure moved: data-trend="up" or "down" draws the arrow; the words say by how much and against what. Variants: data-trend: up, down, flat. Say the direction in words too ("up 12.4% on the last 30 days"), not only by the arrow and its colour.
  `<p data-slot="stat-delta" data-trend="up"><strong>12.4%</strong> vs last 30 days</p>`
- **stat-label** on `<p>` or `<dt>` or `<span>`.A stat's label.
  `<p data-slot="stat-label">Spent</p>`
- **stat-value** on `<p>` or `<dd>` or `<span>` or `<output>`.A stat's figure.
  `<p data-slot="stat-value">€2,315</p>`
- **list** on `<ul>` or `<ol>`.A vertical list of items to read or choose from: conversations, files, results. Items that choose something are buttons or links inside list-item.
  `<ul data-slot="list"><li data-slot="list-item">…</li></ul>`
- **list-item** on `<li>` or `<button>` or `<a>`.One row of a list: an avatar or icon, a title and a quiet line, and something at the end such as a count. As a button it is a choice; aria-current or data-active marks the chosen one. A choosable row is a <button> or <a>; mark the chosen one with aria-current="true".
  `<li><button data-slot="list-item" aria-current="true"><span data-slot="avatar" data-name="Maya Lind"></span><span class="grid flex-1 min-w-0"><span class="font-medium truncate">Maya Lind</span><span class="text-muted-foreground truncate">See you at 7</span></span><span data-slot="badge">2</span></button></li>`
- **avatar** (shadcn/ui) on `<span>` or `<div>`.A person's picture, or their initials from data-name. Round. Variants: data-size: md, sm, lg; data-tone: muted, tint, primary. Decorative beside a name: aria-hidden="true". Alone: role="img" and an aria-label.
  `<span data-slot="avatar" data-name="Maya Lind" aria-hidden="true"></span>`
- **badge** (shadcn/ui) on `<span>` or `<div>` or `<a>`.A small label: a status, a count, a category. Variants: data-variant: default, secondary, destructive, outline, ghost, link, success, brand, chip. A count needs words for a screen reader: aria-label="2 unread".
  `<span data-slot="badge" data-variant="success">Paid</span>`
- **status** on `<span>`.A state in a row: a coloured dot and its word (Paid, Pending, Failed). Quieter than a badge, for tables. Variants: data-tone: default, success, warning, destructive. The word carries the meaning; the dot is decoration.
  `<span data-slot="status" data-tone="success">Paid</span>`
- **kbd** (shadcn/ui) on `<kbd>`.A key to press.
  `<kbd data-slot="kbd">Enter</kbd>`
### States

- **skeleton** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<span>`.A placeholder the shape of what is loading. Size it with utilities (h-4 w-full). aria-hidden="true"; say "Loading" once elsewhere (aria-busy on the region).
  `<div data-slot="skeleton" class="h-4 w-full" aria-hidden="true"></div>`
- **empty** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<section>`.What a list or screen shows when there is nothing yet: an icon or emoji, a title, one line, and the action that fills it.
  `<div data-slot="empty"><h3>No expenses yet</h3><p>Add the first one and the model keeps the totals.</p><button data-slot="button">Add expense</button></div>`
- **alert** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>`.A message about the page: an error (the error state), a success or a note. Holds alert-title and alert-description. Variants: data-variant: default, destructive, success. role="alert" for something that just went wrong, role="status" for news.
  `<div data-slot="alert" data-variant="destructive" role="alert"><p data-slot="alert-title">The model refused the move</p><p data-slot="alert-description">A king cannot move into check.</p></div>`
- **alert-title** (shadcn/ui) on `<p>` or `<h2>` or `<h3>` or `<div>`.An alert's first line.
  `<p data-slot="alert-title">Saved</p>`
- **alert-description** (shadcn/ui) on `<p>` or `<div>`.An alert's explanation.
  `<p data-slot="alert-description">Every total is up to date.</p>`
### Forms

- **choice-group** on `<fieldset>` or `<div>`.A set of choices shown as cards, one per option, laid out with grid utilities: services, plans, stylists, sizes. A <fieldset> with a <legend>; the inputs inside are a radio group (or checkboxes) the keyboard moves through as usual.
  `<fieldset data-slot="choice-group" class="grid gap-3 md:grid-cols-2"><legend data-slot="label">Service</legend>…choice…</fieldset>`
- **choice** on `<label>`.One option as a card: a <label> holding its radio or checkbox input, a title and a quiet second line. The card shows it is chosen. The input is real and inside the label, so a click, the keyboard and a screen reader all work; it is hidden from sight, not from them.
  `<label data-slot="choice"><input type="radio" name="service" value="cut"><span class="font-medium">Haircut &amp; style</span><span class="text-muted-foreground">45 min · £55</span></label>`
- **steps** on `<ol>`.Sections of a form or a flow, numbered in order: each step a numbered circle beside its title and its fields. An ordered list; each step has a heading.
  `<ol data-slot="steps"><li data-slot="step"><h2 data-slot="step-title">Choose a service</h2>…</li></ol>`
- **step** on `<li>` or `<section>`.One numbered step.
  `<li data-slot="step"><h2 data-slot="step-title">Your details</h2>…</li>`
- **step-title** on `<h2>` or `<h3>` or `<legend>`.A step's title, in the display face, level with its number.
  `<h2 data-slot="step-title">Pick a time</h2>`
### States

- **progress** on `<progress>`.How far something has got. A native <progress> with a label.
  `<progress data-slot="progress" value="62" max="100" aria-label="Budget used"></progress>`
### Content

- **table** (shadcn/ui) on `<table>`.Rows of records. Numbers align at the end with data-align="end" on their cells. Header cells are <th scope="col">; give the table a <caption> or a labelled card.
  `<table data-slot="table"><thead><tr><th scope="col">Item</th><th scope="col" data-align="end">Amount</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Rent</td><td data-align="end">€1,100</td></tr></tbody></table>`
### Controls

- **button** (shadcn/ui) on `<button>` or `<a>`.An action. One default button per view; the rest outline, secondary or ghost. Variants: data-variant: default, destructive, outline, secondary, ghost, link, brand; data-size: default, xs, sm, lg, icon, icon-xs, icon-sm, icon-lg, md, xl. A real <button type="button"> (or type="submit" in a form). An icon-only button needs aria-label. The brand variant is the brand accent: at most one per view.
  `<button data-slot="button" data-variant="outline" data-size="sm" type="button">Export</button>`
### Forms

- **field** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<fieldset>`.One form field: label, control, an optional description and the error. data-invalid shows the error state. Variants: data-orientation: vertical, horizontal, responsive. The control has an id the label names, aria-invalid="true" when wrong, and aria-describedby naming the error.
  `<div data-slot="field" data-invalid><label data-slot="label" for="amount">Amount</label><input data-slot="input" id="amount" inputmode="decimal" aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="amount-error"><p data-slot="field-error" id="amount-error">An expense is more than zero.</p></div>`
- **label** (shadcn/ui) on `<label>` or `<legend>` or `<span>` or `<p>` or `<div>`.A control's name, or a group's (give a group's label an id and name the group with aria-labelledby).
  `<label data-slot="label" for="what">Description</label>`
- **field-description** (shadcn/ui) on `<p>` or `<div>`.A hint under a control.
  `<p data-slot="field-description" id="d">In euros.</p>`
- **field-error** (shadcn/ui) on `<p>` or `<div>`.Why the model refused the value. The page shows the model's words; it never decides. Named by the control's aria-describedby; role="alert" when it appears after a submit.
  `<p data-slot="field-error" id="e">An expense is more than zero.</p>`
- **input** (shadcn/ui) on `<input>`.A one-line text control. Named by a <label for>. Not autofocused on load: a View may not take focus without a gesture.
  `<input data-slot="input" id="what" placeholder="Groceries">`
- **textarea** (shadcn/ui) on `<textarea>`.A text control for more than one line; grows with what is typed when data-autosize is set. Variants: data-autosize: set. Named by a <label for>.
  `<textarea data-slot="textarea" id="note" rows="3"></textarea>`
- **select** on `<select>`.A choice of one from a list. Named by a <label for>.
  `<select data-slot="select" id="cat"><option>Food</option></select>`
- **checkbox** on `<input>`.A yes or no, on <input type="checkbox">. Inside its <label>, or named by one.
  `<label data-slot="label"><input data-slot="checkbox" type="checkbox"> Recurring</label>`
- **switch** on `<input>`.An on or off setting that applies at once, on <input type="checkbox" role="switch">. role="switch" and a label.
  `<label data-slot="label"><input data-slot="switch" type="checkbox" role="switch"> Dark sky</label>`
- **slider** on `<input>`.A number from a range, on <input type="range">. Named by a label; aria-valuetext when the number needs words.
  `<input data-slot="slider" type="range" min="0" max="100" aria-label="Volume">`
### Navigation

- **tabs** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>`.Views of one thing, one shown at a time. Holds tabs-list (of tabs-trigger) and one tabs-content per trigger. The runtime sets role=tablist, tab and tabpanel, aria-selected and arrow-key focus. Each trigger names its panel with aria-controls.
  `<div data-slot="tabs"><div data-slot="tabs-list" aria-label="Period"><button data-slot="tabs-trigger" aria-controls="m">Month</button><button data-slot="tabs-trigger" aria-controls="y">Year</button></div><div data-slot="tabs-content" id="m">…</div><div data-slot="tabs-content" id="y" hidden>…</div></div>`
- **tabs-list** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<nav>`.The row of tabs. Variants: data-variant: default, line.
  `<div data-slot="tabs-list" aria-label="Period">…</div>`
- **tabs-trigger** (shadcn/ui) on `<button>`.One tab.
  `<button data-slot="tabs-trigger" aria-controls="m">Month</button>`
- **tabs-content** (shadcn/ui) on `<div>` or `<section>`.One tab's panel.
  `<div data-slot="tabs-content" id="m">…</div>`
### Overlays

- **dialog** on `<dialog>`.A modal question or form over the page, on native <dialog>. A button with data-open="<id>" opens it; data-close or a form with method="dialog" closes it. Native <dialog> traps focus and closes on Escape. Name it with aria-labelledby on its dialog-title.
  `<button data-slot="button" data-open="confirm">Delete</button><dialog data-slot="dialog" id="confirm" aria-labelledby="ct"><div data-slot="dialog-header"><h2 data-slot="dialog-title" id="ct">Delete the expense?</h2><p data-slot="dialog-description">This cannot be undone.</p></div><div data-slot="dialog-footer"><button data-slot="button" data-variant="outline" data-close>Cancel</button><button data-slot="button" data-variant="destructive" data-close>Delete</button></div></dialog>`
- **dialog-header** on `<div>` or `<header>`.Title and description of a dialog or sheet.
  `<div data-slot="dialog-header">…</div>`
- **dialog-title** on `<h2>` or `<h3>`.A dialog's title.
  `<h2 data-slot="dialog-title" id="t">Delete?</h2>`
- **dialog-description** on `<p>`.A dialog's one line of explanation.
  `<p data-slot="dialog-description">This cannot be undone.</p>`
- **dialog-footer** on `<div>` or `<footer>` or `<form>`.A dialog's actions, the main one last.
  `<div data-slot="dialog-footer">…</div>`
- **sheet** on `<dialog>`.A panel that slides in from an edge, on native <dialog>: settings, details, a cart. Opened and closed as dialog is. Variants: data-side: right, left, bottom. As dialog.
  `<dialog data-slot="sheet" data-side="right" id="filters" aria-labelledby="ft"><div data-slot="dialog-header"><h2 data-slot="dialog-title" id="ft">Filters</h2></div>…</dialog>`
- **popover** on `<div>`.Content that opens beside the control that opens it, on the native popover attribute: <button popovertarget="id"> and <div data-slot="popover" popover id="id">. Native popover closes on Escape and on a click outside.
  `<button data-slot="button" data-variant="outline" popovertarget="more">More</button><div data-slot="popover" popover id="more">…</div>`
- **tooltip** on `<div>`.Not written by the page: put data-tooltip="words" on any control, and the runtime shows them on hover and focus. The runtime names the tooltip with aria-describedby; an icon button still needs its aria-label.
  `<button data-slot="button" data-size="icon" aria-label="Undo" data-tooltip="Undo the last move">↶</button>`
- **toast** on `<div>`.Not written by the page: ui.toast("Saved", { variant: "success" }) shows a short message at the corner and lets it go. Variants: data-variant: default, success, destructive. Announced through a polite live region; never the only place a refusal is said.
  `ui.toast("Expense added", { variant: "success" })`
### Conversation

- **thread** on `<ol>` or `<ul>` or `<div>`.The messages of a conversation, oldest first, scrolled to the newest. Holds bubbles; put it in a scroll-area with data-stick="bottom". aria-live="polite" so a new message is read; a label naming the conversation.
  `<ol data-slot="thread" aria-live="polite" aria-label="Messages with Maya">…</ol>`
- **bubble** on `<li>` or `<div>`.One message. data-from="me" sits at the end in the primary colour; "them" at the start on muted. A time or status goes in a small line inside. Variants: data-from: them, me. Say who wrote it in text, for example in a visually hidden span, when sides alone show it.
  `<li data-slot="bubble" data-from="me">See you at 7<time class="sr-only">09:41</time></li>`
- **composer** on `<form>`.The box to write in and its send button, at the foot of a conversation or a prompt screen. Enter sends; Shift+Enter starts a line. A labelled textarea and a send button with a name.
  `<form data-slot="composer"><label class="sr-only" for="m">Message</label><textarea data-slot="textarea" data-autosize id="m" rows="1" placeholder="Write a message"></textarea><button data-slot="button" data-size="icon" type="submit" aria-label="Send">↑</button></form>`
### Data

- **chart** on `<figure>`.A chart the runtime draws from data-values, in the theme's chart colours: data-type="bar", "line" or "donut", data-values="Food:820,Home:1100". Set data-values again and it redraws. Variants: data-type: bar, line, donut, spark. An aria-label that says the numbers; the runtime gives the figure role="img".
  `<figure data-slot="chart" data-type="bar" data-values="Food:820,Home:1100" aria-label="Spending by category: Food 820, Home 1100"></figure>`
### Games

- **board** on `<div>`.A square grid for a board game: data-size="8" for chess, 3 for noughts and crosses. Holds size × size board-cell buttons in reading order. Arrow keys move between cells. Variants: data-pattern: checker, plain. role="grid" with an aria-label; each cell a button whose aria-label says the square and what is on it.
  `<div data-slot="board" data-size="8" data-pattern="checker" aria-label="Chess board">…64 board-cell buttons…</div>`
- **board-cell** on `<button>`.One square. data-shade="dark" for the dark squares of a checker board (the runtime sets it when data-pattern="checker"); data-selected, data-target (a legal place, shown as a dot), data-last (the last move) and data-danger (a king in check) show what the model said. aria-label: the square and its piece, for example "e4, white knight".
  `<button data-slot="board-cell" aria-label="e4, white knight" data-piece="white">♞</button>`
- **table-top** on `<div>` or `<section>`.The felt of a card game: a surface for piles laid out with grid utilities.
  `<section data-slot="table-top" class="grid grid-cols-7 gap-3" aria-label="Table">…piles…</section>`
- **pile** on `<div>` or `<ol>`.A place cards are stacked: stock, waste, foundation or a tableau column. data-fan="down" spreads them so each shows its top. Cards can be dragged from one pile to another, or picked and dropped with Enter; the runtime then sends a ui:move event and the page asks the model. Variants: data-fan: none, down. aria-label naming the pile; the runtime makes each face-up card focusable and announces a pick.
  `<div data-slot="pile" id="t3" data-fan="down" aria-label="Column 3">…playing-card…</div>`
- **playing-card** on `<button>` or `<div>` or `<li>`.A playing card: data-rank (A, 2–10, J, Q, K), data-suit (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs) and data-face (up or down). The runtime draws its corners from those. Variants: data-face: up, down. The runtime names each card from what its corner shows, so the visible text is in the name ("Q, Queen of hearts"); a face-down card is "Face-down card".
  `<button data-slot="playing-card" data-rank="Q" data-suit="hearts" data-face="up"></button>`

## A theme of the app's own

When a person names a look, write it as tokens in `app/design/theme.json` (outside `view/`; the build compiles it in) and the words of the choice in `app/DESIGN.md`:

```json
{
  "schema": "prism/theme/1",
  "name": "Starfleet",
  "modes": [
    "dark"
  ],
  "dark": {
    "background": "#05070d",
    "foreground": "#e6f1ff",
    "primary": "#ff9d00",
    "primary-foreground": "#05070d",
    "brand": "#ff9d00"
  },
  "radius": {
    "md": "0.25rem",
    "lg": "0.375rem",
    "xl": "0.5rem"
  },
  "font": {
    "display": "Orbitron, Archivo"
  }
}
```

`check` refuses a theme whose text is under 4.5:1 on its surface.

## What the gate refuses

- `design.system`: The page uses a component that needs prism-ui.js without loading it.
- `design.class`: A class that is not in the vocabulary: an arbitrary value such as p-[13px], a palette colour such as bg-red-500, or a name that does not exist.
- `design.icon`: A data-icon that is not one of the catalogue's icons.
- `design.slot`: A data-slot that is not a catalogue component, one put on an element it is not made for, or a variant it does not have.
- `design.color`: A colour written as a value (hex, rgb(), hsl(), oklch(), a named colour) in the page's HTML, CSS or script.
- `design.value`: A size, space, radius or duration written as a value in the page's own CSS, or var() of something that is not a token.
- `design.font`: A font family outside the theme, an @font-face or @import, or a stylesheet from elsewhere.
- `design.inline-style`: A style attribute, a <style> element, or a script that sets a style to a raw value.
- `design.fork`: A rule of the page's own stylesheet that targets a component's data-slot: a local copy of a component.
- `design.import`: An import between the page's files, which a holospace cannot follow.
- `design.contrast`: A pair of text and surface in the theme under 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA).
- `design.theme`: design/theme.json is not a prism/theme/1 theme.
